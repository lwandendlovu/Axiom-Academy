FAQ

Questions parents ask

Is the first session really free?

Yes. The first diagnostic session is free. It shows where the gaps are, so we can recommend the right programme.

How do online lessons work?

Lessons run on Google Meet or Microsoft Teams with a shared digital whiteboard. A phone works, but a laptop or tablet is better.

Where are in-person lessons held?

In KwaZulu-Natal, at a safe, open venue such as a library or community hall. We confirm the venue when we book.

How do I pay?

By EFT or card. Monthly packages are paid before the first session. Please give 24 hours' notice to reschedule.

Do you guarantee marks?

No honest tutor can. What we do promise is a clear plan, consistent effort and honest progress reports, and that is what improves marks.

Do you offer help for learners who can't afford fees?

We keep some places at reduced or no cost for learners in need. Mention it in the form and we'll talk.
